Formulary RxNorm Enrichment — v5

Instructions

This tool enriches your formulary.json with RxCUI and RxNorm concept names from the NLM RxNorm public API. Run it once on desktop Safari or Chrome.

1. Click Load formulary.json — select from iCloud or Downloads.
2. Optionally click Re-process T2/T3 & Concentration Mismatches to clear suboptimal prior matches.
3. Click Start Enrichment — runs ~25-35 min for full formulary.
4. When complete, click Download and commit to GitHub.

Safe to pause and resume — enriched formulations are skipped on reload. Log shows T1 (green) / T2 (orange) / no-match (--) per formulation.
